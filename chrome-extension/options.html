Trail Forecast for AllTrails

On a trail page, click the extension's button (or press Alt+Shift+S) to save that trail to your trails. You stay on the page; a ✓ on the button means it's saved. Right-click the button for Save and open the forecast. Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

The live dashboard is https://aaronmaddenjk.github.io/oregon-weather-dashboard/ (the default). For your local copy while developing, use http://localhost:8000/. Saved trails stay with whichever address you use.

Handy for working through an area: open the trails you want in tabs, then press Alt+Shift+S on each one, or Alt+Shift+A to save every trail tab in this window at once (ones already saved are skipped). Or collect links in a notepad file and use your trail list (also on the button's right-click menu) to open them a batch at a time.

Your trails live in your private wx-trails repo. Paste the same key you connected in the dashboard (Map → Trails) so the button can show ✓ on trails saved from any device. It stays in this browser's extension storage and is only sent to GitHub. Without it, ✓ shows only for trails saved from this browser. Saving works either way (the dashboard does it).
