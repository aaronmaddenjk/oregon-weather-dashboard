Trail Forecast for AllTrails

On a trail page, click the extension's button (or press Alt+Shift+S) to save that trail to your trails. You stay on the page; a ✓ on the button means it's saved. Right-click the button for Save and open the forecast. Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

The live dashboard is https://aaronmaddenjk.github.io/oregon-weather-dashboard/ (the default). For your local copy while developing, use http://localhost:8000/. Saved trails stay with whichever address you use.

Handy for working through an area: open the trails you want in tabs, then press Alt+Shift+S on each one. Or collect links in a notepad file and use your trail list (also on the button's right-click menu) to open them a batch at a time.
